About your guide

Rooted in Lushoto

Kibwana Hussein Ngoda is a local guide based in Lushoto, in Tanzania's Usambara Mountains. He grew up between these forests and farms and knows the trails from the Magamba rainforest to the viewpoints above the Maasai plain.

Every walk, trek and drive is arranged around your pace — a half-day hike, a village-to-village overnight trek, or a full cultural program with accommodation at St Eugene Lodge.

  • Based in Lushoto, P.O. Box 268
  • English-speaking local guide
  • Half-day to multi-day programs
  • Transport, permits and meals arranged
